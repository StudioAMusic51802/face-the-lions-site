Our Mission

Face The Lions Studio exists to make music and art that outlives the moment. Every lyric my own, written from the heart, for the one person who needs to hear it. And through my photography, to show the beauty God put in this world that people walk right past. I don't create for numbers — I create because God gave me something to say and something to see, and if it reaches one person, it's done its job. Sight is not the same as vision.
