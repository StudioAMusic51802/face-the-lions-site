About Chuck

Legally blind. Still seeing clearly.

I'm Chuck Roelofs, a photographer, songwriter and builder, and the man behind Face The Lions Studio. I'm legally blind. People hear that and assume the story ends there. It doesn't. Sight is not the same as vision.

The lion in the name is the promise: face the obstacles, and fight back.

Behind the camera, I chase the light most people walk right past: a sunset through the trees, frost on a lake, one flower in the grass. In 2020, FOX 9 shared my story, and the message hasn't changed: don't let anyone tell you you can't do something.

My music comes out as CGR Creative. Faith-driven metal and hard rock, with some rap and R&B when the story calls for it, and every lyric is my own. My songs play on Body of Belief Radio, and “He Chose Me” is on their Christmas album.

I also design and build web apps, because creating doesn't stop at one medium.

Most of all, I'm a husband, a dad and a grandpa, and a believer. Every win here belongs to God.

Our Mission

Face The Lions Studio exists to make music and art that outlives the moment. Every lyric my own, written from the heart, for the one person who needs to hear it. And through my photography, to show the beauty God put in this world that people walk right past.

I don't create for numbers. I create because God gave me something to say and something to see, and if it reaches one person, it's done its job.
